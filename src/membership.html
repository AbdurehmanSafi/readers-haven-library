Membership Plans

Whether you are a student, an adult reader or a whole family, Readers' Haven has a plan for you. The fees and rules below are the library's proposed annual membership plans.

Student Membership

PKR 1,000 / year

  • Annual fee: PKR 1,000
  • Borrowing limit: 3 books at a time
  • Loan period: 14 days
  • Late-return rule: PKR 10 per book per day
Most Popular

Adult Membership

PKR 2,500 / year

  • Annual fee: PKR 2,500
  • Borrowing limit: 5 books at a time
  • Loan period: 21 days
  • Late-return rule: PKR 20 per book per day

Family Membership

PKR 5,000 / year

  • Annual fee: PKR 5,000
  • Borrowing limit: 10 books at a time (up to 4 members)
  • Loan period: 28 days
  • Late-return rule: PKR 15 per book per day

These are proposed plans. Late fines never exceed the price of the book. Lost or damaged books are charged at replacement cost.
